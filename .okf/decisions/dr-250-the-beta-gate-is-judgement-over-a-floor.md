# A trigger nobody watches is a date nobody wrote down

Canon §10.11 was deferred on 2026-08-03 *until M5 lands*, and owed before M6-J5. M5 closed on
2026-09-07 and §13 went on saying the question *"owes it to M5, not to now"* for five weeks. A
deferral keyed to an event needs somebody to notice the event; the sync audit did, by reading the
deferral against the milestone marks rather than against itself.

# A wrong fact is not an unknown

The floor's second limb counts a printed value that disagrees with the document it came from. A `—`
printed for an unknown is G4 working, not a bug ([G4](/invariants/g4-unknowns-are-counted.md)).

# Left open on purpose

How such a bug is marked on the board is M9-D's decomposition. Who the three integrators are is
`#362`'s question: DR-006 made them internal users and DR-036 made the project public without saying
whether that moved RISK-08's population back.

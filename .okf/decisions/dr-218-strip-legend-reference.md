Settled by the user on 2026-10-02, building M6-H (`#347`); the defect is `#971`.

# What was ruled

- **The legend reference prints in row 1 of the ANSI A strip**, right-aligned before the sheet
  title.
- docs/08 §2.1's *"under the active scheme"* is corrected: CR-023 rules the id, `T0.01`.

# Why

Whole House's second row was 708 pt on a 690 pt strip and the reference was last on it. Row 1 has
about 480 pt free on every starter; leading row 2 would cut the firm's contact instead, and row 3
has about 15 pt spare.

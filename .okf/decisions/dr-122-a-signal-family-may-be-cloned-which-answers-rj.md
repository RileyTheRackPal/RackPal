# In one line (docs/10)

**A signal family may be CLONED from a shipped one**, which satisfies RJ-012's objection rather than overruling it: the clone inherits every field a rule reads, so there is no blank form and no hand-rolled family. PAL-1 moves from CI into the app for this case, since a user-created family does not exist when CI runs. Written to the project **and** the shelf, the project copy authoritative. Connector-type and colour-scheme editors stay refused

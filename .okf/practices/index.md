# Practices

Working conventions that earned their place; each is here because it was tested
by something going wrong.

* [A field added beside a documented one inherits nothing](a-field-beside-a-documented-one-inherits-nothing.md) - the comment explaining the neighbour's contract is four lines up and applies word for word, so nobody asks which lines ENFORCE it; a live draw survived a switch to another plan for four chapters.
* [A test that asserts an absence names its due date](a-test-that-asserts-an-absence-names-its-due-date.md) - three came due in one task and cost five minutes between them, because each comment said which item would retire it.
* [A deferred action needs a frame](defer-past-the-gesture.md) - addPostFrameCallback queues but does not schedule, and a TabBarView settles the page under a tab switch made mid-click.
* [Assert the artifact, not the mechanism](assert-the-artifact-not-the-mechanism.md) - a test counted corridors, passed, and the sheet painted a fifth of its ink twice.
* [A modelled field is not a reachable one](a-modelled-field-is-not-a-reachable-one.md) - fields with no writer, a callback with no caller, `NetworkConfig` whose only writer is the JSON decoder, and three in one milestone where the missing producer was the seed catalogue's own `p()`; all three surfaced the same way, as a rule firing on a connection that is correct.
* [Check a decomposition for holes](check-a-decomposition-for-holes.md) - a healthy-looking four-task issue had nothing that read a CSV file; ask what produces each task's input, and know a gap from a scope change.
* [A dimension sits in an inequality](a-dimension-sits-in-an-inequality.md) - a 22 mm gang pitch against a 33.34 mm opening is two holes overlapping; the check is free, and the reading was real but was answering a different axis.
* [Census before building](census-before-building.md) - print the field over the whole corpus first; it has caught an empty field, an unmeasured cost, a wrong ordering, and a field that RESOLVES perfectly while collapsing 48 templates onto 9 of 17 marks.
* [Decompose before building](decompose-before-building.md) - the first task of a milestone is its own breakdown, and task descriptions carry the traps.
* [Diagnose before fixing](diagnose-before-fixing.md) - twelve fix commits touched neither cause; what that means and what shape of fix held.
* [The ZIP trap](the-zip-trap.md) - how a file arrives decides whether it is trusted; a property that lives in the channel cannot be gated at the source.
* [Two documents agreeing is not verification](two-documents-agreeing-is-not-verification.md) - three fields named by a ruling AND a design document and present in neither the model nor the codec; a citation is evidence of intent, not of existence.
* [One keymap registry](one-keymap-registry.md) - every binding is a table row, arbitrated across every platform, and why a real collision does not throw.
* [Re-read a task against canon before pricing it](re-read-a-task-against-canon.md) - two of M3-D's four tasks asked questions that rulings dated after them had already answered; the cost that does not announce itself is the estimate.
* [Trade objections are questions, not findings](trade-knowledge-beats-workflow-reasoning.md) - three rulings have overruled an objection grounded in how the work happens in the field; the corpus is silent about the trade, so a workflow claim can be confidently wrong and pass every check here.
* [A test's exemption list is where the bug lives](a-tests-exemption-list-is-where-the-bug-lives.md) - a chip loop checked two of twelve cases and a comment excused the rest; the excused ones held a chip that did not parse.
* [A rule you invent must be checked against the good cases](a-rule-you-invent-must-be-checked-against-the-good-cases.md) - a gate written from one wrong bank name disproved itself on `hdmi`; the distinction was English, not structure.
* [Measure the control before choosing it](measure-the-control-before-choosing-it.md) - a 55-row dropdown in a 76-point column renders every row as an ellipsised stump; the three sibling columns using the same widget were not evidence, because their labels fit.
* [Drive the keyboard, not the finder](drive-the-keyboard-not-the-finder.md) - nineteen widget tests drove the builder form and missed that Tab went through the section rail, because `enterText` teleports and a person walks; a navigation defect presents as a data defect.
* [A spec table is an illustration until censused](a-spec-table-is-an-illustration-until-censused.md) - four M4 tasks in a row found a derived document's table was examples; the port grid's named nine columns with no model field and missed eight fields with no column.
* [Ask what produces each task's input](ask-what-produces-each-tasks-input.md) - five tasks built a grid, its columns, its packs, its dots and its footer, and not one of them created a port.
* [Ask what consumes each task's output](ask-what-consumes-each-tasks-output.md) - M4-D built a template save, gated it, tested it, and no surface in the tree read what it wrote; a milestone's exit criterion was unperformable behind a comment asserting it.
* [Measure at the size the criterion will meet](measure-at-the-size-the-criterion-will-meet.md) - the schematic met its one-frame budget at 300 links and missed it by 5.8x at 3,600; every property that made the fixture good also made it comfortable, and the gate stayed green because the larger size was never asked.
* [Print the output before claiming copy is done](print-the-output-before-claiming-copy-is-done.md) - a `contains` assertion cannot see grammar, density, or a sentence that has quietly become false; three instances now, and the last was a refusal still announcing a limit four commits after it was lifted.
* [A stated cost is a hypothesis until it is measured](a-stated-cost-is-a-hypothesis.md) - four times a figure or a LIST in a ruling or an issue decided what got built and measuring changed the answer; the cost justifying a deferral is the least measured, because deferring excused measuring it.
* [A non-nullable field with a default has already answered](a-defaulted-enum-has-already-answered.md) - G4 protects the silence in a nullable field; an enum with a constructor default has none, so writing nothing is a claim, and every generated C14 said it had no gender.
* [A string no test names is a string nobody has read](a-string-no-test-names.md) - a click on a palette row announced "Released outside the rack ... was not placed" for a gesture nobody made, and a grep found no test naming that message in EITHER direction; zero hits is a finding, not a coverage gap.
* [A wrong ruling ID resolves](a-wrong-ruling-id-resolves.md) - PLAN-D cited AC-058/D12 for image hardening in four places and D12 is localisation; a dangling ID fails to resolve and a colliding one is caught by scanning refs, but a WRONG one resolves, so every check passes and the trail back to the real obligation is gone.
* [A borrowed rule is not re-checked](a-borrowed-rule-is-not-re-checked.md) - "already-compressed rasters do not deflate" is true of PHOTOGRAPHS and false of line art on white, which is what this product receives; it shipped behind four confident sentences, one of which argued for nothing, and the test read the `if` back rather than the data.
* [Writing UNVERIFIED next to a decision is not pricing it](flagged-is-not-handled.md) - PLAN-D/1 recorded two unknowns in capitals and both were fatal; an honest flag reads as a risk already handled, so it buys the decision the same passage it should have blocked. Sort them: an unknown that could REVERSE the choice is a blocker, not a note.
* [Audit what a reuse claim OMITS](audit-what-a-reuse-claim-omits.md) - an estimate justified by "the machinery already exists" carries a list, and auditing the list is the cheap half; PLAN-E/1 found four of five named pieces reused verbatim, the fifth deleted work rather than adding it, and every hour went to three pieces nobody had named.
* [A test that hangs produces no failure to diagnose](a-test-that-hangs-is-worse-than-one-that-fails.md) - Picture.toImage and instantiateImageCodec never complete inside testWidgets' fake-async zone, so the file emits NOTHING and reads like a slow suite; two runs were killed before the cause was a hypothesis. Silence from a test runner is a symptom, not a wait.
* [When a gate refuses finished work, check the boundary first](a-gate-can-move-a-chapter-boundary.md) - PLAN-E/3 built a callback, a menu item and two green tests, and callbacks-have-callers refused the lot; the call site could have been reworded until the scanner matched. The gate was right, the gesture was unreachable, and the work belonged in a later chapter.
* [A mutation that does not build measures nothing](a-mutation-that-does-not-build-measures-nothing.md) - PLAN-E/4's most important mutation printed SURVIVED because the edit named a type the file does not import, so nothing ran. Zero failing tests is the same output for "the suite is blind" and "the suite never started", and the structural mutations you actually care about are the ones that stop compiling.
* [A gate's scope is part of its claim](a-gates-scope-is-part-of-its-claim.md) - the reachability gate refused a finished callback one chapter and missed an entire unreachable feature the next. It scans function-typed fields on *Actions records in ONE package; the importer was free functions in another, in a file named plan_import_actions.dart. Read the scan, not the name, and census by call site when you need the answer.
* [A null collaborator in a test is a configuration](a-null-collaborator-in-a-test-is-a-configuration.md) - every case in the plan canvas's own file passed `marquee: null` because they predate the band, and the only thing that mounts that canvas in the app passes one; the armed-pin branch was unreachable in the configuration that ships and reachable in the one every test used. The hole is in the PAIRING of two coherent test files, not in either.
* [Run the real input before writing the tests](run-the-real-input-before-writing-tests.md) - the PDF lifter refused all 18 pages of the first real plan set with a plausible CONTENT refusal while the parser was fine, and no hand-written fixture could have found it because a fixture small enough to write by hand has its `endobj` inside the 64 KB bound. The second real set then agreed with the fixtures exactly, which is what makes this a rule of thumb rather than a law. The separating check is whether the refusal is right for the RIGHT REASON: ask what the code had to get right to reach that message.
* [A property proven by a fixture's default is not proven](a-fixture-default-is-not-a-proof.md) - every plan fixture in rackpal_ui is built without a scale, so every pin gesture the suite ran was already a pin on an uncalibrated plan and passed for a reason nobody had named, while a pin on a CALIBRATED plan was exercised nowhere in the package. Ask which fixture field carries the property, then write the case that sets it the other way, through the gesture rather than the model.
* [A brief expires, and the census that checks the tree will not notice](a-brief-expires.md) - three of PLAN-G's four briefs had gone stale in four weeks, and the one that mattered was a RULING made two days after the decomposition. Reading a task against the tree finds the first kind of drift and is structurally blind to the second, because the tree is exactly where an unapplied ruling has left no trace. Diff the canon against the brief's own date, and do not assume the later ruling wins.
* [A guard the model makes unreachable is not defence in depth](a-guard-the-model-makes-unreachable.md) - a redundant `<= 0` beside a null check SURVIVED being deleted, because PlanScale already refuses the state. When a mutation survives, ask whether the branch is reachable before asking what test is missing: one conclusion adds a case, the other deletes a line. An uncoverable branch makes the file's mutation score a lie and makes the next reader doubt the invariant.
* [A number in a ruling is still a measurement](a-normative-number-is-still-a-measurement.md) - DR-152 was ruled on a band cap of "roughly 180 rows" that measured 60, which is the exit criterion's own rough-in, so the overflow it called "expected never to fire" fired on the sixty-first drop of an ordinary house. A wrong figure in a normative clause is not a stale copy of a truth held elsewhere: for a while it is the only copy, and it propagates as agreement into every file that cites it. Run the arithmetic before the clause is written, pin it in a test, and let the prose say what the number MEANS rather than what it is. The tell is a superlative attached to an unmeasured figure.
* [A vendored binary needs a control a package brings with it](a-vendored-binary-needs-its-own-control.md) - a pub dependency arrives with a lockfile hash and a resolver that refuses a mismatch; a committed binary is megabytes of opaque bytes that no reviewer reads and no diff shows. Vendoring is the same dependency with its integrity check REMOVED, so the pin, the test that recomputes the hash and the licence in the tree are what rebuild it. The test cannot tell a good binary from a malicious one: it tells you nobody swapped it after the day it was pinned. And the bootstrap run has nothing to verify against, so a script that fetches, hashes and commits in one motion has reinvented the build-time download it was meant to refuse.
* [A surface tested alone has never met its host](a-surface-tested-alone-has-no-host.md) - the Outlets tab had two green files and neither mounted it in the product: one pumps the tab with no editor above it, the other pumps the editor and only asserts `Outlets` is IN THE TAB STRIP, never switching to it. The strip was tested as an index and never as a route, and the first thing ever to open the tab inside the app threw on its first frame at a defect that shipped with the tab: a `ListTile` paints on the nearest `Material` ANCESTOR, and the editor's `ColoredBox` had been swallowing every splash and selected tint on the plate rail since M3-H/2. Ask which test MOUNTS a surface, not which test names it; one mount is the whole fix.
* [A gate's output is read as a fact](a-gate-is-read-as-a-fact.md) - two of the reachability gate's twelve findings were bugs in the GATE, and both became board items with DONE WHEN clauses on its word alone; `#880` asserts a class is declared in a file that declares nothing, and one `cat` settled it. The sharper half: the identical bug was recorded in that gate's own header pointing the other way, fixed on the edge side of the scan and left on the node side. A census has at least two sides, what it counts as a THING and what it counts as a REFERENCE to one, and fixing either teaches you nothing about the other. Run both passes and diff them: here it was one line of Python and the answer was 73 against 72.
